# Sound effects

All three sounds come from Kenney's **Interface Sounds** pack (version 1.0). They are converted from Ogg Vorbis to
16-bit WAV, renamed for the scenes, and start with 50 ms of added silence (see `LEAD_IN` in
`src/components/sfx.tsx`).
